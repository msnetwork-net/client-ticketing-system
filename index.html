About MS NETWORK

Headquartered in Kunnummal, Malappuram, MS NETWORK started over a decade ago with a simple mission: providing high-quality, budget-friendly, and customer-centric IT and networking services. Through sheer technical expertise and friendly, transparent client care, our team has grown exponentially over the last 10+ years.

What began as a localized team in Malappuram has now expanded its operational footprint across all 14 districts in Kerala, as well as establishing strong operational presence in major economic and tech hubs including Bangalore, Chennai, Mumbai, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.
